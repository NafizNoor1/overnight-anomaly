# FOMC Scheduled Meeting Announcement Dates

- **Source:** Federal Reserve official pages — https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm (2021-2026) and https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomchistorical{YEAR}.htm for 1994-2020.
- **Coverage:** January 1994 through June 2026 (first date 1994-02-04, last date 2026-06-17); each date is the statement-release day, i.e. the final day of the scheduled meeting.
- **Scheduled meetings only:** unscheduled meetings, conference calls, and notation votes are excluded; the cancelled March 17-18, 2020 meeting is excluded (2020 therefore has 7 entries); the special Sept 15, 2003 session (no statement) is excluded.
- **Retrieved:** 2026-07-14.
